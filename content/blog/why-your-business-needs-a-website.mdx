**Short answer:** a website gives your business a place online that you fully own, where customers can find you on Google and in AI assistants, check that you are trustworthy, and contact you, book or buy at any hour of the day.

Many businesses start with a Facebook page, an Instagram account or a WhatsApp number. Those are useful, but they are rented space. A website is the foundation the rest of your online presence points back to.

## Why a social media page is not enough

Social platforms are excellent for reaching people, but they come with limits:

- **You do not control the rules.** Platforms change their algorithms, features and policies, and your reach can drop overnight.
- **Search engines rarely show them first.** When someone searches for a service in your city, Google and AI assistants look for clear, well-structured websites to recommend.
- **It is harder to look established.** A professional site with your own domain name signals that you are a serious, stable business.
- **Your content is not really yours.** If an account is restricted or hacked, you can lose years of work.

A website does not replace social media. It gives social media somewhere to send people.

## Four things a website does for your business

### 1. It helps customers find you

People look a business up before they call or visit. A fast, well-built website is what Google, Google Maps and AI assistants such as ChatGPT read when they decide which businesses to show.

### 2. It builds trust

Clear information about what you offer, where you are, your prices or starting prices, and how to reach you answers the questions customers have before they contact you.

### 3. It works while you sleep

A website can answer common questions, show your products, and take bookings, orders or enquiries around the clock, even when your phone is off.

### 4. It is an asset you own

Everything you publish on your own domain keeps working for you. Good pages can bring in customers for years.

## Seven things every good business website should have

1. **A clear headline** that says what you do and who you do it for, in plain words.
2. **Your services or products**, each with a short explanation and, where possible, a price or starting price.
3. **Contact options that are easy to use**, such as a WhatsApp button, a phone number and a simple form.
4. **Proof that you are real**: photos of your work, client reviews, and your location or service area.
5. **Fast loading on phones.** Most visitors will be on a mobile device, often on mobile data.
6. **Search setup done properly**: page titles, descriptions, a sitemap and structured data, so search engines and AI assistants understand your business.
7. **Security and reliability**: HTTPS, regular backups and a host that keeps the site online.

## How much does a website cost?

It depends on what it needs to do. A simple site that presents your business and lets people contact you is a much smaller project than an online store with payments or a booking system with customer accounts. The best approach is to start with what brings results now, then add features as the business grows.

## The next step

If you are deciding whether a website is worth it, ask yourself one question: how are new customers finding you today, and how many are you missing because they cannot find you online?
