**Short answer:** AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) mean writing and structuring your website so that Google's answer boxes, AI Overviews and AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude can understand your business, trust it, and quote it correctly.

Traditional SEO is about ranking in a list of links. AEO and GEO are about being **the answer**.

## Why this matters now

More people now ask questions instead of typing keywords: "who builds websites in Buea?" or "which app helps manage a vehicle fleet?". Answer engines reply with a short summary and a few sources. If your website is clear and well structured, your business can be one of those sources.

## Seven steps that work

### 1. Write a clear definition of your business

Write one or two sentences that say exactly who you are, what you do and where. Use the same wording everywhere: your website, social profiles and directory listings. Consistency helps engines connect the facts to your business.

### 2. Answer real questions directly

Use headings phrased as the questions customers ask, and answer them in the first sentence or two. A short, quotable answer followed by detail is ideal. A good FAQ page is one of the most useful pages you can publish.

### 3. Add structured data

Structured data (JSON-LD using schema.org) is a small block of code that tells machines the facts plainly: your name, what kind of business or person you are, your location, services, contact details and profiles. Useful types include Person or Organization, Service, FAQPage, BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList.

### 4. Make every page readable without JavaScript

Many AI crawlers do not run JavaScript. If your content only appears after scripts load, they may see an empty page. Static or server-rendered HTML solves this.

### 5. Let AI crawlers in

Check your robots.txt file. Make sure you are not blocking crawlers such as Googlebot, Bingbot, GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot, unless you have a reason to.

### 6. Publish an llms.txt file

An llms.txt file is a plain-text summary of your site written for AI systems: who you are, what you offer and links to your key pages. It is a simple, emerging convention that makes your most important information easy to find.

### 7. Show freshness and proof

Keep pages up to date and show a "last updated" date. Add real client reviews, case studies and links to your work. Engines prefer sources that look current and credible.

This site is built to follow every step above:
